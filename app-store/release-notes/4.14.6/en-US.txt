Ordered lists keep their numbers in order: add or delete an item and the rest renumber themselves.

Bold, italic, strikethrough and code over several paragraphs now format each paragraph on its own, instead of one pair of markers that showed up as literal asterisks.

Menus: Find and Replace in the Edit menu, the zoom commands in a View › Zoom submenu, and the Help menu lists the Markdown cheatsheet, keyboard shortcuts and the command line separately. The Insert menu gains headings 4–6.

Links in rendered notes always open in your browser, never inside SoloMD.

After a few days of use, SoloMD asks once — and only once — whether you'd like to star it on GitHub.
