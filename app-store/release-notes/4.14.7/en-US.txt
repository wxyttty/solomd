Pick any folder in the Files app as your workspace — iCloud Drive, On My iPhone or another app's folder. SoloMD remembers it across launches.

SoloMD's own folder now shows up in the Files app under On My iPhone › SoloMD.

Notes opened from the Files app are edited in place: saving updates the original instead of making a copy.

Rename, move and delete files in the file tree: long-press a file or tap ⋯.

Folders you opened before an update open again, instead of saying they are no longer there.

SoloMD from the App Store sends no usage data, and asks for a rating only with Apple's own rating sheet.
